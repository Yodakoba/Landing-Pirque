Four tiers of culinary experience for your clients

Price per person. Tap the photo for more detail. You'll find the full menu below.

See the full menuThe same in all four experiences

Starter

Country board (1 per 4 people): sopaipillas, pan amasado, homemade pebre, homemade nachos and flavoured butter.

Main course

1 portion of 300 g or 2 of 150 g, over charcoal and wood: Angus flank, Pacific salmon, ribeye or striploin cooked in the embers, Patagonian lamb, picanha in red wine, pork ribs or roast chicken.

Sides

2 options per guest: vegetables cooked in the embers, native Chilote potatoes, chips or boiled potatoes, sweetcorn pastelera, white rice, potato salad, Chilean salad, green leaves, quinoa salad, cochayuyo seaweed ceviche or creamed spinach.

Dessert

1 option: mote con huesillo, seasonal fruit, pancakes with manjar, leche asada, milhojas cake or apple crumble.
